# Content checklist (business owner)

The site never publishes invented claims. These items need your input or approval before launch.

## Please review

- [ ] Homepage, service pages and About copy (`src/content/*.ts`, `src/app/(site)/**/page.tsx`).
- [ ] Commitments stated on the site — change anything you don't want to promise:
  - "written scope and fixed quote" after the consultation (process step 1, principles)
  - "usually within one to two working days" reply time (Start a project page)
  - typical timeline of 4–6 weeks for a new website (process timeline)
  - deposits on larger projects (FAQ)
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms (`src/app/(site)/privacy`, `src/app/(site)/terms`). These are a
      sensible starting point, not legal advice — have them checked for your jurisdiction.

## Please supply

- [ ] A public contact email for the privacy policy and structured data (`site.contactEmail`).
- [ ] Profile links: LinkedIn, GitHub, Upwork, etc. (`site.sameAs`).
- [ ] A professional photo and a short bio for the About page (optional but recommended).
- [ ] Real projects you have permission to show: client name (or anonymised), services, challenge,
      approach, verifiable outcome, year, optional link and images. The Work section appears
      automatically once at least one case study is published (`src/content/work.ts`).
- [ ] Testimonials only if genuine and attributable, with the client's permission.

## Rules

- No guaranteed rankings, invented metrics, fake logos, awards or testimonials.
- Illustrative graphics stay labelled "Illustration".
- Don't create location pages unless the offer genuinely differs for that market.
